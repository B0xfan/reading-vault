# Saving words

**Pro.** Part of Reading Vault Pro.

Found a word worth keeping? Save it, and it becomes a note in your vault, with its meaning and the sentence where you found it.

![The lookup card with the Save word button](images/word-lookup.png)

## Save a word

1. Look up a word. See [Word lookup](word-lookup.md).
2. On the lookup card, press **Save word**.

Reading Vault makes one note per word in a Words folder inside your Reading folder. The note has:

- the word and its meaning
- the sentence you found it in, with a link to the book
- the "Explain in this sentence" answer, if you asked for one

## The same word in another book

Save a word you've already saved, from a different book, and that book's sentence is added to the same note. The word then belongs to both books.

## See your saved words

- On a book's page, the **Words** list shows every word you saved from that book. Click one to open its note.
- All your words are regular notes in the Words folder, so you can search, link and review them like any other note.

## Good to know

If Pro ever lapses, words you already saved stay saved. They're your notes.
