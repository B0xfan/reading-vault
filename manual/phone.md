# On your iPhone or iPad

**Pro.** Reading Vault on a phone or tablet needs a Pro license.

On a computer, the free version keeps working as before. One Pro license covers your computer and your phone, with no extra charge.

## What you need

- An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or newer.
- Obsidian on the phone, with the same vault as your computer (Obsidian Sync, iCloud or whatever you already use to sync). Your books, highlights and notes come across with the vault.
- Reading Vault turned on in the phone's Obsidian: Settings, Community plugins.

## Unlock Pro on the phone

Without Pro, the phone shows one screen: "Reading Vault on phones is part of Pro".

1. Copy your key from your purchase email. It starts with **RVPK-**.
2. Paste it into the box on that screen and tap **Unlock**.

Your key is checked on the phone itself. It never goes online.

## What works on the phone

- Your Library, reading EPUB and PDF books, highlights, notes and bookmarks, laid out for a small screen.
- To highlight or look up a word, touch and hold it (drag the handles for a longer passage), then wait a moment: the color panel appears, with **Look up** when one word is selected.
- **+ Add books** opens the phone's Files app, so you can pick an EPUB or PDF from there.
- PDFs open in **Text** view, which lays the words out to fit your screen. Tap **Page** at the top to see the printed page instead.
- In Export highlights, **Save file** offers **Save into my vault** and **Share**.

## Listening on the phone

- The phone reads aloud with its own built-in voices. The natural offline voices are for computers only.
- Listening stops when you lock your phone. Keep the screen on while you listen.
- The speed steps are set for the phone's voices, so 1.3× on a phone sounds about like 1.5× on a computer.

## Android

Reading Vault installs on Android, but it doesn't support Android yet. It shows a short message instead of your books. Your books and highlights stay safe in your notes.
